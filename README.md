# ⚡ Energy Insight Card

> Transform your Home Assistant energy data into actionable insights.

Energy Insight Card is a modern Lovelace card for Home Assistant designed to go beyond traditional energy dashboards.

Instead of simply displaying charts, it helps you understand:

- 📈 How your energy consumption evolves
- 💰 How much it costs
- 🔍 Why it changes
- 📅 When important events occurred
- 🤖 What anomalies or trends can be detected

---

## ✨ Features

### Dashboard

- Daily consumption
- Cost estimation
- Comparison with yesterday
- Weekly and monthly trends

### Visualizations

- Hourly histogram
- Daily comparison
- Monthly comparison
- Annual heatmap

### Insights

- Consumption anomalies
- Trend detection
- Seasonal analysis
- Significant changes

### Timeline

Add important events like:

- Pool installation
- Heat pump
- Solar panels
- Electric vehicle
- Water heater replacement

and instantly visualize their impact.

---

## Philosophy

Most dashboards answer:

> **How much energy did I consume?**

Energy Insight Card answers:

> **Why did my consumption change?**
